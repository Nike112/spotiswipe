The problem we’re solving

            

              Music discovery can become repetitive. Listeners need a quick way
              to express their taste and create a varied playlist that fits the
              time they have. Spotiswipe combines swipe feedback, ranked
              recommendations and playlist-level constraints.
            

            
Why Amazon Personalize?

            

              Amazon Personalize learns from item interactions to recommend
              relevant items to a particular user. Our local service models its
              custom workflow: datasets → solution versions → evaluation →
              campaign deployment → recommendations → new events.
            

            

              Our transparent algorithm combines item-to-item collaborative
              filtering, genre preferences, mood matching and popularity. It is
              an educational simulation, not a reproduction of Amazon’s
              proprietary recipes. AWS’s resource infrastructure, autoscaling
              and exact API schemas are outside the simulation.
            

            
Our additional enhancement

            

              Mix studio optimizes a playlist as a set. It balances relevance,
              novelty and genre coverage, while enforcing a duration ceiling and
              a maximum number of tracks per artist. We compare it both with
              plain relevance ranking and with relevance ranking under the same
              artist limit, making the extra contribution of discovery easier to
              inspect.

What the evaluation means

            

              We hold out each eligible listener’s last positive item, remove
              its earlier occurrences from that listener’s training data, and
              report Hit Rate and NDCG at 5 against popularity. The small
              catalog and synthetic histories make this a functional test, not
              evidence of real-world accuracy.
            

            
Data, playback and privacy

            

              The catalog contains real CC0 recordings with bundled 30-second
              excerpts. Full track durations are used for mix budgets; the
              player previews excerpts. Source pages link to the complete
              recordings. Energy and valence are manually curated on a 0–1
              scale, not extracted Spotify audio features. Artwork is original
              procedural artwork.
            

            

              Profiles, swipes, trained versions and saved mixes stay in
              IndexedDB on this device. There is no account login or
              cross-device sync. The website can cache its app and previews for
              offline use. Spotify content is not used by this recommendation
              model; the original app remains in the repository for reference.
