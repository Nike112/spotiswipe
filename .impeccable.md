## Design Context

### Users

Music listeners exploring unfamiliar tracks through swipes; faculty inspecting a cloud-service simulation. Desktop use should support focused listening and comparison; phone use should prioritize quick preview and keep/pass actions.

### Brand Personality

Playful, direct, curious. An independent record shop with the speed and clarity of a modern music app. Dark listening-room identity, warm lime accents, and distinctive geometric record sleeves.

### Aesthetic Direction

Ink and olive-neutral surfaces, warm off-white text, locally bundled Manrope typography, original SVG sleeves with separate genre palettes. Restrained borders, generous spacing around music, compact controls. Avoid giant marketing introductions, repeated generic record artwork, and equally prominent technical controls.

### Design Principles

- Put music and play/keep/pass controls in the first screen at ordinary laptop and phone sizes.
- Use bottom navigation and a persistent compact player on phones; keep profile creation and audio settings available.
- Let artwork carry expressive color; reserve the main accent for actions and selection.
- Keep Personalize analysis in the lab or expandable detail.
- Preserve data provenance and honest simulation boundaries.
- Provide labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and clear feedback.
