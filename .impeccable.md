## Design Context

### Users

Music listeners exploring unfamiliar tracks through swipes; faculty inspecting a cloud-service simulation.

### Brand Personality

Playful, direct, curious. Preserve the creator's existing dark pixel-inspired direction as the default pending their response to the design question.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark record-store interface with pixel-inspired display accents, warm cream text, muted green actions, generous spacing and readable body typography.

### Design Principles

- Discovery first, technical detail progressively disclosed.
- Make synthetic data and simulation limits explicit.
- Every score and comparison must have a traceable calculation.
- Accessible labels, keyboard controls, responsive layout and reduced motion support.
